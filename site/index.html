From your drawer to a student's desk

Everything we collect goes straight to the Electronic Recycling Association. Their technicians and volunteers wipe it, fix what's fixable and load a clean operating system, and the finished machine is given to a charity, school or care home that asked for one. What's genuinely past saving gets broken down for parts and materials instead of going to landfill.

questions

Before you
hand it over

Does any of this cost me anything?

No. Pickups, drop-offs and shipping labels are all free — ERA funds the program through refurbished equipment sales, partnerships and donations.

Do I need to wipe my device first?

No. ERA erases every drive with data destruction software, and physically shreds anything that can't be wiped. Just take out SIM cards, SD cards and anything else you want to keep.

What if it's broken, cracked or won't turn on?

Still bring it. Dead batteries, smashed screens and missing chargers are all fine — technicians strip usable parts from anything they can't repair, and the rest is recycled for materials.

I only have one old laptop. Is that worth it?

Yes. One working laptop is one student who starts the year with a computer, and one less device in landfill. There's no minimum.

Can I ask for physical destruction instead of a wipe?

Yes. ERA offers on-site and off-site shredding on mobile hard-drive shredders. Contact ERA for hard drive shredders
